Tab order

The nine tabs always stay; you can only move them. Ctrl+number keeps opening the same tab.

What a theme can change

Colours, fonts, text size, corner radius, shadows, gradients and the order of the tabs. Everything else is ignored: no new buttons or text, nothing hidden or moved, no scripts, no web links, and the tabs and app functions stay as they are. The app tells you which lines it ignored.

Order book depth (read-only, display only)

Hong Kong, Tokyo, London, Frankfurt and other listed markets use their home exchange book when your IBKR account has that data.
Run Futu OpenD (or moomoo OpenD) and log in there; your Futu login stays in OpenD and the app never sees it. Covers Hong Kong, US, Shanghai/Shenzhen (Stock Connect) and Singapore books that your Futu quote rights include (HK depth needs LV2). If OpenD has RSA encryption switched on, switch it off: the app talks to it on this PC only.
Hong Kong, US, Shanghai/Shenzhen and Singapore books, as far as your Longbridge quote level allows. Access tokens expire (you set how long when you make one); paste a new one here when it does.
US, Hong Kong, China A and Singapore books your Tiger quote permissions include. The app never asks for your account number or trading password.
Nasdaq and NYSE books from Schwab's streamer (US stocks only). Schwab makes you sign in again every 7 days. After sign-in the browser shows an error page at 127.0.0.1; that's expected, copy its address.

Direct exchange feeds (US stocks)

When one is set, it replaces the broker's book for US stocks it covers; the broker above stays the fallback and still covers other markets.
TotalView covers every stock that trades on Nasdaq, including NYSE-listed ones; OpenBook is used for NYSE-listed stocks. Nasdaq's and NYSE's own feeds are licensed to firms (Nasdaq Cloud Data Service is a Kafka stream that needs a Nasdaq data agreement), so the app reads the same exchange data through Databento, which licenses it per user and bills by data used. A TotalView subscription you already have at IBKR works through IBKR → Nasdaq TotalView above.

Keys are encrypted with your Windows account and only used by the app's background process; the page never sees them. Yahoo data comes from unofficial endpoints and can change without notice.
